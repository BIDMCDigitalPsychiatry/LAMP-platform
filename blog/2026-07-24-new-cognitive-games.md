Fourteen new cognitive assessments have completed development and will be available to teams on the BIDMC-hosted server on **Wednesday, July 29**. They span attention, working memory, processing speed, executive function, spatial cognition, language, and decision-making, and each produces structured, analysis-ready data.

<!-- truncate -->

**Running your own server?** Email mindlamp@bidmc.harvard.edu and we'll walk you through adding them.

## The new games

Each game links to its full documentation, including scoring and data output.

**Processing speed**
- **[Simple & Choice Reaction Time](/activities/reference/simple-rt)**: psychomotor processing speed and decision time, combining the two classic Donders reaction-time paradigms.

**Attention & cognitive control**
- **[Stroop Color-Word Test](/activities/reference/stroop)**: selective attention, cognitive control, and interference suppression (Stroop, 1935).
- **[Eriksen Flanker Task](/activities/reference/flanker)**: attentional control and conflict resolution; a core component of the NIH Toolbox Cognition Battery and the Attention Network Test.

**Working memory**
- **[N-Back](/activities/reference/nback)**: working-memory updating, scored with signal detection theory (d-prime and criterion).
- **[Memory Match](/activities/reference/memory-match)**: visual-spatial recognition memory and working memory.

**Executive function & planning**
- **[Tower of London](/activities/reference/tower-of-london)**: executive function and planning (Shallice, 1982).
- **[Wisconsin Card Sorting Test (WCST)](/activities/reference/wcst)**: cognitive flexibility, set-shifting, and abstract reasoning, with perseverative-error scoring.
- **[Water Sort](/activities/reference/water-sort)**: planning and problem-solving, in the Tower of London / Tower of Hanoi family.
- **[Sliding Puzzle](/activities/reference/sliding-puzzle)**: spatial problem-solving and planning, with move efficiency scored against the mathematically optimal solution.
- **[Nonogram](/activities/reference/nonogram)**: logical deduction and constraint satisfaction.

**Spatial cognition**
- **[Mental Rotation](/activities/reference/mental-rotation)**: spatial visualization ability (Shepard & Metzler, 1971).

**Language**
- **[Lexical Decision Task](/activities/reference/lexical-decision)**: lexical access speed and word/nonword discrimination (d-prime).
- **[Letter Logic](/activities/reference/letter-logic)**: vocabulary, hypothesis testing, and logical deduction (color-coded five-letter word guessing).

**Decision-making**
- **[Delay Discounting](/activities/reference/delay-discounting)**: temporal impulsivity, quantified with both Area Under the Curve and hyperbolic-k indices.
